Landscapes shaped by nature

Distinctive living environments where beauty, biodiversity and food production belong together.

A different vision of landscaping

Every landscape has the potential to become more than an ornamental garden. By working with natural processes, we can create places that are beautiful, ecologically resilient and rich in life.

Native and locally adapted trees, wild edible species, fruit trees, herbs and ornamental vegetables can form a landscape that serves people while respecting the character of its surroundings.

Principles of a living landscape

Adapted to its place

Plant choices respond to local climate, soil, water and existing vegetation, supporting resilience through environmental extremes.

Beauty with purpose

Trees, herbs, flowers and edible plants combine visual richness with biodiversity and the potential to produce food.

Working with nature

Natural processes guide the design, with the aim of reducing unnecessary artificial inputs and demanding maintenance.

A considered approach to each property

No two properties are alike. The process begins by understanding the land—its natural conditions, existing vegetation, possibilities and limitations. From there, a landscape concept can be developed around the particular character of the place and the owner's aspirations.

The guiding principle is simple: create landscapes that belong to their environment and grow in harmony with it.
